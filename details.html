Full Description

Tucked into a serene Bel Marin Keys setting, this refreshed 2 bedroom, 2.5 bath end-unit townhome offers approximately 1,216 sq ft of bright, comfortable living with an easy indoor-outdoor flow. The main level features newer luxury vinyl plank flooring and a remodeled kitchen with quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, a built-in microwave, wine refrigerator, and a substantial butcher-block island with seating. The kitchen opens to the dining area and a living room with fireplace, where sliding glass doors lead to a private fenced patio with paver hardscape, perfect for outdoor dining or relaxing. Upstairs are two spacious en-suite bedrooms with vaulted ceilings, upper-level laundry, and additional flex or storage space accessed by a drop-down ladder. One carport space plus one uncovered parking space are included.

Location

Bel Marin Keys is a waterfront community on Novato's east side, built around a network of lagoons and surrounded by open space and marshland. Bermuda Harbor sits right at the entrance to the Keys, a short drive from Highway 101, shopping, and dining.
